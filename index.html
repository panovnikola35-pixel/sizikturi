How net is calculated

Revenue is the credit note total. Net contribution subtracts imported fuel costs and recorded tolls. This is a management estimate, not accounting or tax advice.

Currency: EURFuel values use the EUR gross amount column if present; otherwise EUR net amount. Review VAT treatment before relying on totals.

Privacy & limitations

Parsing happens in your browser using public CDN libraries. No backend is included. A PDF containing only scanned images cannot be text-extracted here. This app does not provide official tax/accounting determinations.
